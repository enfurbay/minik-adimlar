Where is the Parent Area?

Tap the parent button on the home screen and answer the question written out in words on the screen (a multiplication or a number) by entering digits. Settings, child profiles, the progress report, the time limit, backup and purchases are all in this area.

What does the Full Version unlock?

The 8 games on the first page, the first level of all other games, the stories, the sticker book and the garden are free. The Full Version unlocks all remaining levels with a single one-time payment. There are no subscriptions and no ads; the purchase applies to all child profiles on the device.

How do I get my purchase on a new phone?

Tap Parent Area › Full Version › Restore purchase. If you are signed in with the same Apple Account, it unlocks without paying again.

Will progress be lost?

While iCloud backup in Parent Area › Backup is on (the default), profiles, stars, stickers, the garden and pictures are backed up to your family's own iCloud account. When you switch phones or reinstall the app, the progress comes back. For this, the device must be signed in to iCloud.

I don't want my child to leave the app

You can turn on Guided Access in your iPhone's Settings › Accessibility. You can also limit daily play time in Parent Area › Play time.

Refunds
