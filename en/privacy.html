Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No account
  • No data collected

In short

Lalina: Little Steps was made for young children. The app shows no ads, does not require an account, contains no analytics or tracking software, and sends no information about your child to us or to third parties. We do not operate any server, so we hold no data about you or your child.

Information stored on your device

For the app to work, the following information is kept only in the app's own storage area:

  • The child's name (optional; it can be left blank or a nickname can be entered), age group and the animal picture they chose
  • Stars earned, levels completed, and notes on topics the child found difficult
  • Stickers, the garden layout and colored-in pictures
  • Daily play time and the settings chosen by the parent

This information is not sent to us; we cannot access it.

iCloud backup

While “iCloud backup” in the Parent Area is on (it is on by default), profiles and progress are backed up through Apple's iCloud service to your family's own iCloud account and synced between your devices that use the same Apple Account. This backup is kept on Apple's infrastructure, in your account; we cannot see it.

You can turn off backup at any time in Parent Area › Backup. When you turn it off, the backup in iCloud is deleted and the progress on the device remains. Apple's privacy policy applies to how iCloud works.

Purchase

The app offers a single one-time “Full Version” purchase; there are no subscriptions. Payment is made through Apple's App Store. Payment and card details are processed only by Apple; the app neither sees nor stores them. The purchase screen is behind the parental gate.

What we don't collect and permissions we don't request

  • No permission is requested for location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or notifications.
  • No advertising identifier, device identifier or usage statistics are collected.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics or social media software is used.
  • External links in the app (such as this page) are placed only behind the parental gate.

Internet use

All games, sounds and images are built into the app; it can also be played in airplane mode. The internet is used for only two things: iCloud backup and App Store purchases. Both are handled by Apple.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from children under 13, does not track them and does not show behavioral advertising. This approach was chosen to comply with Apple's rules for the Kids category, Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), the GDPR and COPPA in the United States.

Deleting information

  • When you delete a profile in the Parent Area, all information belonging to that profile is deleted from the device and from the iCloud backup.
  • The “Reset progress” option deletes only the selected child's progress.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the records on the device but does not delete the backup in iCloud. To delete the backup as well, turn off backup before uninstalling, or delete the app's data in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated.
